# Artificial Colourant Detection in Foods, Beverages, Powders and Traditional Sweets

### A Smart Food Safety Screening and Awareness System

## Project Overview

Artificial food colourants are commonly used to enhance the appearance and attractiveness of food products. However, excessive or unauthorized use of these colourants can raise food safety concerns. Identifying artificial colouring in everyday food products is often difficult for consumers, as conventional testing methods require specialized laboratory facilities.

This research project aims to develop a **smart, accessible and user-friendly food colourant screening system** that helps consumers assess potential artificial colouring in commonly consumed food products using images captured through a smartphone.

The proposed system focuses on four major food categories:

- **Cooked Foods:** Yellow rice, dhal, curries and gravies.
- **Powdered Foods:** Chilli powder, turmeric powder and curry powder.
- **Fruit Beverages:** Fruit-based drinks and beverages.
- **Traditional Sweets:** Kesari, Muscat and Boondhi.

Each category is analysed according to its unique visual characteristics to provide relevant screening results.

## How the System Works

The user captures or uploads an image of a food product through the mobile application. The system identifies the relevant food category, analyses its visible colour characteristics and provides an appropriate screening result.

Depending on the food category, the system aims to:

- Identify visual indicators associated with potentially artificial colouring in cooked foods.
- Screen powdered food products for suspected colour adulteration.
- Assess the possible use of artificial colouring in fruit beverages.
- Estimate artificial-colour intensity in traditional sweets using **Low, Medium and High** levels.

The application also provides understandable results and relevant food-safety awareness information to help users make more informed decisions.

## Project Objectives

- Make preliminary food colourant screening more accessible to everyday consumers.
- Support the identification of suspicious artificial colouring across different food categories.
- Provide simple and understandable screening results.
- Increase public awareness of artificial food colourants and potential food safety concerns.
- Encourage informed food choices and responsible food consumption.
- Develop a convenient, non-destructive screening solution for everyday use.

## Expected Outcome

The expected outcome is an integrated mobile-based food colourant screening and awareness system that enables users to assess selected food products quickly and conveniently.

By bringing together screening capabilities for cooked foods, powdered foods, fruit beverages and traditional sweets, the project aims to provide a unified platform that promotes **food safety awareness, consumer confidence and informed decision-making**.

## Important Note

This system is intended for **preliminary screening and educational awareness only**. Its results do not confirm the exact presence, identity or concentration of artificial colourants and should not be considered a replacement for laboratory testing or official food safety assessments.
